# Code of Conduct

Unsaved Form Bundle is maintained for a welcoming, useful PHP and Symfony community. Everyone is welcome to ask questions, report problems, review code, or contribute, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Critique proposals and code, never the person submitting them.
- Respect other people's privacy and avoid sharing sensitive information.
- Make room for newcomers and different approaches.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Where this applies

These rules apply to GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and other interactions carried out on behalf of this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a private conduct concern, contact the maintainer at **alkinbg@gmail.com** with the subject **UnsavedFormBundle — Code of Conduct**. Include links or context when possible. Do not post personal details publicly.

The maintainer will consider the circumstances and may ask for clarification, moderate or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation. No fixed response time is promised.

**Security vulnerabilities are different:** please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use private vulnerability reporting instead of a public issue.
